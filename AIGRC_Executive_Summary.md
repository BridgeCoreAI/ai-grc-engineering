# AI GRC Engineering Portfolio — Executive Summary

**Author:** Grace M. Adjeli — BridgeCore AI LLC
**Repository:** github.com/BridgeCoreAI/ai-grc-engineering

---

## What this is

A complete, engineering-first portfolio translating the three major AI governance frameworks — NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and the EU AI Act — into operational controls, workflows, and automation. It is built to demonstrate a specific, uncommon skill set: the ability to move AI governance from policy documentation into systems that are actually operated, measured, and audited.

## Why it exists

Most AI governance work stops at the policy layer — a written framework, a set of principles, a compliance checklist. This repository exists to close the gap between that documentation and execution: what happens when a policy has to become a workflow, a workflow has to produce evidence, and evidence has to survive an audit.

## What's inside

**Three frameworks, fully documented.** NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and the EU AI Act are each broken into module-by-module explanations (9 modules per framework), supporting diagrams, and — for NIST — a set of quick-reference cards covering trustworthy AI properties, AI lifecycle stages, risk types, and human oversight levels.

**A working intake workflow.** `templates/intake/ai-intake-workflow.md` is a production-ready template for triaging AI systems as they enter an organization's governance pipeline.

**Five automation scripts.** Python tooling that operationalizes GRC tasks directly: risk rating, risk-tier classification, risk register generation, evidence staleness checking, and evidence packaging for audit.

## How it fits the broader practice

This public repository is the demonstrable, teachable layer of a larger body of work. A private companion repository extends it with client-facing tooling and applied consulting artifacts not published here. Together, they reflect a practice built around one governing idea: governance is not what is defined, it is what is enforced at execution.

## Who this is for

- **Hiring managers and clients** evaluating applied AI governance engineering capability, not just framework familiarity.
- **Practitioners and learners** looking for a structured, first-principles walkthrough of NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, and the EU AI Act.
- **Auditors and reviewers** who want to see how framework requirements translate into version-controlled, evidence-producing systems.
