# AI GRC Engineering Portfolio, Executive Summary

**Author:** Grace M. Adjeli, BridgeCore AI LLC
**Repository:** github.com/BridgeCoreAI/ai-grc-engineering

---

## What this is

An engineering-first portfolio working through the three major AI governance frameworks, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and the EU AI Act, module by module in complete depth. It is built to demonstrate a specific, uncommon skill set: the ability to work through AI governance frameworks with the rigor needed to eventually move from policy documentation into systems that are actually operated, measured, and audited.

## Why it exists

Most AI governance work stops at the policy layer, a written framework, a set of principles, a compliance checklist. This repository exists to build the depth of framework understanding that closing that gap requires: what a framework actually specifies, function by function and clause by clause, before any of it can become a workflow, produce evidence, or survive an audit.

## What's inside

**Three frameworks, fully documented.** NIST AI RMF (8 modules), ISO/IEC 42001 (9 modules), and the EU AI Act (9 modules) are each broken into complete, module-by-module explanations of their structure, functions, and requirements. NIST AI RMF also includes four quick-reference cards covering trustworthy AI properties, AI lifecycle stages, risk types, and human oversight levels.

## What's not in this repository yet

Earlier planning for this portfolio described a working intake workflow template and a set of Python automation scripts (risk rating, risk-tier classification, risk register generation, evidence staleness checking, and evidence packaging). Neither currently exists in this repository. This summary previously described them as present; that was inaccurate, and this section replaces that claim with the actual current state. If and when they're built, this section will be updated to reflect that.

## How it fits the broader practice

This public repository is the demonstrable, teachable layer of a larger body of work: 26 complete framework modules across three major governance frameworks, plus supporting reference material. A private companion repository extends it with client-facing tooling and applied consulting artifacts not published here. Together, they reflect a practice built around one governing idea: governance is not what is defined, it is what is enforced at execution.

## Who this is for

- **Hiring managers and clients** evaluating applied AI governance engineering capability, not just framework familiarity.
- **Practitioners and learners** looking for a structured, first-principles walkthrough of NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, and the EU AI Act.
- **Auditors and reviewers** who want to see how framework requirements are broken down in operational depth.
